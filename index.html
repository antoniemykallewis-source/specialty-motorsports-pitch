What we found

A FEW THINGS. Worth a closer look.

01

Hours do not match.

See the second site ↗

Your main site says 6:00 PM; a dealer-branded site says 5:30 PM. Conflicting hours can make shoppers unsure about visiting.

02

Too many competing tools.

See the homepage ↗

Trade-in, payment and chat tools surround the inventory page. Extra choices can distract someone ready to ask about a bike.

03

A heavy phone experience.

See Google’s test ↗

Google’s October 20 phone simulation scored 23/100; its separate real-visitor report passed. Weak connections may still leave shoppers waiting.

How we would help
