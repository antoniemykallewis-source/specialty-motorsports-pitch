What we found / 1 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Ads disagree on closing time.

Active Meta ads list 5:30 PM and 6 PM; the main and separate dealer-branded sites also differ. That uncertainty can cost an after-work showroom visit. Secondary-site ownership is unconfirmed. See the active ads ↗

02

44.4 seconds in the phone test.

October 20 homepage: simulated slow 4G main content took 44.4 seconds; separate real-visitor data was 2.1 seconds and passed Core Web Vitals. Long waits can lose shoppers before they inquire. See the phone test ↗

03

The homepage misses your real story.

Eight image slides, no video embed: shoppers cannot immediately hear bikes or meet staff. That’s a missed chance to build confidence before visiting. Meta ads already include video. See the homepage ↗

What we found / 2 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Your heading misses the dealership.

The main heading is for questions; inventory messaging relies on graphics. Clear Sacramento dealership text would help shoppers and search engines understand what you sell. See the homepage ↗

05

Competing tools interrupt the inquiry.

Trade/payment tabs, a bottom bar and chat surround the inventory journey. Ready buyers must choose between promotions instead of one clear route to call, text or visit. See the homepage ↗

06

Measure buyers, not just clicks.

Meta and two Google tracking containers appear; call and text attribution needs account-level checking. Unclear inquiry tracking can misdirect ad spend. No tracking failure or duplicate events established. See the site setup ↗

How we would help / 1 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01

One set of hours, everywhere.

Confirm closing times, correct active ad copy, and align Google and website listings. Verify who owns the secondary dealer site before updating or retiring it.

02

Make the paid-click path lighter.

Work within DX1 to compress images and delay nonessential widgets. Rebuild the agreed pages, then test actual campaign destinations as well as the homepage while protecting the strong real-visitor result.

03 + 04

Bring the real dealership forward.

Film staff-led bike stories and buying advice. Add tap-to-play video, a clear Sacramento heading and useful category copy that supports both ads and local search.

How we would help / 2 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

05

Give ready buyers a clear next step.

Keep essential accessibility. Simplify competing promotions and make one call, text or inventory inquiry route obvious on each campaign page.

06

Show which ads bring buyers.

Confirm tag ownership, check events and connect consent-aware call, text and form reporting. Judge creative by qualified inquiries with the sales team, not clicks alone.
